The letter

An Open Letter to The NVIDIA Board of Directors

  1. 01 Gratitude
  2. 02 Concern about fairness
  3. 03 Three demands
  4. 04 A timely public answer

To the members of the NVIDIA Board,

We are current and former NVIDIA employees, partners, shareholders, and supporters. We write with gratitude for Jensen Huang and with a clear demand to the Board: bring Jensen back while an independent investigation establishes the facts, unless and until you can provide compelling evidence that his removal is in NVIDIA's best interests.

For thirty-three years, Jensen poured his heart and soul into building NVIDIA. He bet it on ideas that most of the industry dismissed, carried it through years when the bets looked foolish, and stayed long enough to see them change how the world computes. Many of us joined because of him. Many of us stayed because of how he treated the work: as something worth getting right, together, at whatever hour it took. This company is the work of his life. That deserves more than gratitude after the fact. It deserves fairness now.

Our understanding is that an outside investigation has been announced but has not yet concluded. We are concerned that Jensen was removed without meaningful due process. We do not have access to the Board’s private deliberations. We have not been shown evidence substantiating the allegation that the woman at the center of this controversy was a spy. Suspicion or association alone is not a sufficient basis for ending Jensen's leadership. The inquiry must establish the facts, not merely justify an outcome already imposed.

We ask the Board to take three actions:

  • Reinstate Jensen as CEO. Bring him back while an independent investigation establishes the facts. If the Board believes his continued absence is necessary, provide a specific, evidence-based explanation of why it serves NVIDIA’s best interests.
  • Ensure an independent investigation and a fair hearing. Disclose the inquiry’s scope, independence, and expected timeline, and give Jensen a meaningful opportunity to respond before reaching a final decision.
  • Provide a timely public explanation. Explain the concerns, the evidence supporting them, and how employees’ and shareholders’ interests were weighed. Respect legitimate confidentiality without using it to withhold all explanation.

Thirty-three years of leadership do not place anyone above accountability. But accountability requires evidence, a fair hearing, and a reasoned decision. Jensen deserves due process, not mob justice.

Thank you, Jensen. We stand with you.

Signed by the current employees, former employees and shareholders listed below, each speaking for themselves. This letter is independent and is not endorsed by NVIDIA or by Jensen Huang.
